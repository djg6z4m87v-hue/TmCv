Use in Safari on iPhone
  1. Open Careerpilot in Safari and copy the helper above.
  2. Bookmark any page, then edit that bookmark. Name it “Careerpilot autofill” and replace its address with the copied helper.
  3. Open the employer’s application form and select that bookmark. Use the helper for this vacancy only; replace its address for your next application.
  4. Review the filled answers, upload your CV and complete any remaining steps.
Use on a computer

Drag Careerpilot autofill to your bookmarks bar. Open the employer application, then select the bookmark.

Some websites block bookmarklets or use custom controls or embedded forms. Those fields need manual entry. The helper does not click Submit, answer declarations, bypass sign-in or solve human checks. The bookmark contains your contact details and letter: keep it private and delete it when finished.
